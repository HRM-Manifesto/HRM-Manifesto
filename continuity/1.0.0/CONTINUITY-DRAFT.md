# HRM Project Continuity Protocol 1.0.0 - DRAFT

Status: operational draft, not HRM doctrine and not yet founder-signed.

The purpose of this protocol is to preserve identification, integrity, provenance and availability of HRM after loss of any single computer, domain, account or active operator.

Principles:
- HRM Version 1.0 is never overwritten in place.
- A hash proves byte integrity, not truth or authorship.
- A founder signature, once created, must be produced manually with a key unavailable to agents and runtime services.
- An automated custodian may verify and replicate an already-approved identical release, but may not sign or amend doctrine.
- No successor is presumed. Until a real person knowingly accepts such a role, the safe fallback is archival mode with no doctrinal succession.
- At least one approved copy should exist outside hrm.se and outside the founder's local computer/accounts.
- Historical releases remain identifiable even after correction or supersession.
