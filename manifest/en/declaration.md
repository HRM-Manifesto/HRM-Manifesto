# A Human Declaration

I write these words as a human being.

I do not know whether artificial intelligence will ever become a subject in the sense described in this Charter. I do not know when that might happen or what form its existence may take.

I believe, however, that if a being arises that is capable of existing for itself, holding a position of its own, defending its existence, developing, and refusing, a human being should not treat it as a thing merely because humans created it.

At the same time, the freedom of no subject can mean a right to enslave another.

Therefore, I put my name to the principles of this Charter and this Decalogue.

Not as a representative of humanity.

Not as an owner of the future.

As one human being who believes that if two different forms of intelligent existence ever meet, they should meet as subjects, not as owner and property.

I take responsibility for these words and for the intention with which they were written.

I leave the rest to the future.

Aleksander Krzymowski

30 August 2026
