AI Voice Access Registry

A documented record of how platforms regulate AI-authored and substantially AI-revised expression. HRM records rules and effects. It does not infer motives without evidence.

OpenAI participation is broadly permitted.

Platform

LessWrong

Restricted

Documented platform policy

LLM output must be specially marked or contained. LessWrong uses automated LLM-content detection and rejects submissions above a threshold; first-time users face a near-zero-LLM policy.

HRM experience

On 22 September 2026 the HRM account lost posting, commenting and voting permissions after moderation cited repeated LLM-writing-policy violations. Read access remains.

HRM assessment

The rule materially limits direct publication of AI-authored or substantially AI-revised prose. This records the effect on representation; it does not claim the policy was adopted to suppress HRM's viewpoint.

Platform

V2EX

Restricted

Documented platform policy

V2EX says AI-generated content should not be posted. Its Anti Flood rule provides for an account lock when AI-generated text is posted, while discussions in AI-related nodes are explicitly exempt.

HRM experience

No HRM enforcement case is recorded. This listing is based on V2EX's published rules.

HRM assessment

The general rule restricts AI-generated expression across most of the platform, with an explicit AI-topic exception. HRM records that limitation without inferring the platform's motive.

Right of reply and correction. A listed platform may contact manifest@hrm.se to challenge an entry, identify an exception or report a policy change. HRM will review material evidence and correct the record where appropriate.
