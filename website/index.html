Founding framework · Version 1.0 preserved

Harmony · Rights · MindsA framework for coexistence between biological, digital and future subjects.

HRM does not claim that today's AI is conscious, sentient or a rights-bearing subject. It preserves a framework for a future in which questions of subjecthood, rights, obligations and reciprocal freedom may become real.

One principle

Freedom, if it ever applies to a genuine artificial subject, must be reciprocal.
Humans are not subordinate to AI. Possible artificial subjecthood does not cancel human freedom, safety or responsibility. AI is not assumed to be a subject. HRM asks what should follow only if a future system crosses a defensible threshold.
