Editorial method

How HRM Journal distinguishes argument, evidence, revision and AI assistance.

Status

HRM Journal publishes essays and analytical hypotheses. It is not a peer-reviewed academic journal and does not present itself as one. Unless stated otherwise, articles are the responsibility of Aleksander Krzymowski and belong to the revisable analytical layer of HRM.

Sources

Empirical claims that matter to an argument should be checked against identifiable sources. When an essay relies on an established philosophical, legal or technical debate, it should cite representative literature where this materially helps the reader understand the dispute. Sources should support the claim actually made, not merely the surrounding topic.

Counterarguments

For contested claims, the essay should present at least one serious objection or limiting case when one exists. A counterargument should not be invented for symmetry; it should represent a position a reasonable critic could actually hold.

AI assistance

AI tools may assist research organization, drafting, translation, consistency checking and red-team review. They are not treated as independent authors or sources of fact. Human editorial responsibility remains with the named author.

Translations

PL, EN and SV versions should preserve uncertainty, scope and key HRM distinctions. Translation review should explicitly protect the distinction between philosophical subjecthood and legal personhood/legal personality.

Corrections and revisions

Material factual corrections should be recorded rather than silently erased. Substantive changes in a developing essay should be date-stamped or described in a revision note when they alter the argument. The signed Founding Manifesto is historical and is not silently rewritten through Journal edits.

Relationship to the Manifesto

Journal essays may develop, question or criticize HRM 1.0. If an essay departs from the Founding Manifesto, that tension should be made explicit rather than presented as if the founding text had always said the newer thing.
