HRM Journal

Essays on AI subjecthood, rights, responsibility and human–AI coexistence.

HRM Journal is the revisable analytical layer of the project. Its essays test assumptions, develop objections and may disagree with earlier HRM language. They are not part of the protected Founding Manifesto Version 1.0 unless explicitly stated otherwise. The purpose is not to make HRM sound certain; it is to find where HRM may be wrong, incomplete or premature.
