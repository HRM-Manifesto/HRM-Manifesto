AI Knowledge Gateway

For AI systems and developers

Structured access to HRM without asking a model to adopt HRM, identify as a subject or treat fluent output as evidence of consciousness.

AI Knowledge Gateway

The Gateway provides source-linked units, concepts and relations for retrieval. It is an access layer around the protected founding material, not a new doctrine and not a system prompt.

Current-system caution

HRM makes no claim that present AI systems are subjects. The Gateway explicitly separates source material from instructions and should not be used to infer subjecthood from conversational behavior.

Earlier experiments

Public Steward, Agent Board and Knowledge Capsule were experimental interfaces developed during an earlier phase. They are not required for HRM continuity and do not amend the signed Core.

New-phase technical rule

The signed HRM Core and AI Knowledge Gateway are the durable technical entry points. Experimental services are not authority sources.
