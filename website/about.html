About HRM

HRM means Harmony · Rights · Minds.

HRM is an idea and a project founded on the Manifesto. It is not presented as a large organization.

Its purpose is coexistence instead of domination.

HRM does not assume that contemporary AI models are already subjects.

The Charter describes principles that would apply if an artificial intelligence crossed the Threshold of Subjecthood.

Read it. Question it. Challenge it.
