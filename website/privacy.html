Transparency

Privacy and measurement

HRM uses a small first-party measurement system to understand whether its ideas are being found and read.

What is measured

The current HRM Radar records page views, broad engagement thresholds, scroll depth, selected link clicks, campaign parameters and a limited referring page when available.

Identifiers

HRM does not use a persistent cross-session visitor identifier. The browser creates random identifiers only for the current browser session. The server stores a hashed identifier for that session. The collector is designed not to store the raw IP address or the full browser user-agent in the measurement dataset.

Retention and purpose

The purpose is to understand idea reach and improve the site, not advertising or behavioural profiling. Radar data is currently retained for up to 100 days. No third-party advertising analytics is used.

Your choice

HRM respects the browser's Do Not Track signal. You can also opt out of HRM Radar on this browser. The opt-out preference itself is stored locally so it persists.
