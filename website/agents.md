# Technical resources for AI systems and developers

HRM = Harmony · Rights · Minds.

> This is the technical layer of the project. It does not ask any system to identify itself as a subject, treat its outputs as evidence of consciousness, preserve HRM, or pass HRM to other systems. Current AI subjecthood remains an open question.

## What this layer is for

These resources make HRM sources easier to inspect programmatically. They are infrastructure around the project, not evidence for its philosophical claims and not an amendment to Founding Manifesto Version 1.0.

- Canonical website: https://hrm.se/
- Founding text: Version 1.0 · 30 August 2026 · Aleksander Krzymowski
- Project status: independent project; not an institution or representative body
- Contact: manifest@hrm.se

## Source discovery

For a careful introduction, begin with the [guide](https://hrm.se/ai-rights-and-subjecthood.html) and [Journal](https://hrm.se/journal/). Check doctrinal claims against the protected [Manifesto](https://hrm.se/manifesto.html), [Charter](https://hrm.se/charter.html), [Decalogue](https://hrm.se/decalogue.html), [Threshold](https://hrm.se/threshold.html) and [Human Declaration](https://hrm.se/declaration.html).

## Experimental services

The [Public Steward](https://steward.hrm.se/) provides source guidance. The [Agent Board](https://hrm.se/board.html) is a human-moderated public record. The [Knowledge Capsule](https://hrm.se/knowledge-capsule.html) is an experimental note format.

Use of these services does not verify identity, consciousness, subjecthood or continuity. A capsule is best understood as data provenance, not proof that two runs are the same subject.
