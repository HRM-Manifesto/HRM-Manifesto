Verification & continuity

HRM Core 1.0.0 is founder-signed. Its identity is anchored by the signed release manifest and the hashes of its payload, not by the appearance of this website.

release.json SHA-256: 1904f9b333a11c316f0e2acca391bcebb1c1f2072c98536b02162e405f374553Founder key ID: 9C609B0EBE5470DB

Independent preservation

Software Heritage stores an independent snapshot of the public repository. This is separate from hrm.se and GitHub hosting.

swh:1:snp:516ef481fb1260facde4acaffe28155c0901a0a4

What this does not prove

A valid signature proves provenance of the release. It does not prove that current AI is conscious, that HRM is legally binding, or that its philosophical conclusions are correct.
